NeurIPS 2026 BCI (`neurips_bci`): Causal Multimodal Electroencephalography Decoding

Electroencephalography (EEG) records electrical brain potentials from electrodes resting on the scalp, measured in microvolts (uV, millionths of a volt). A brain-computer interface (BCI) translates those scalp signals directly into computer commands without muscle movement. In Track 2 of the 2026 NeurIPS EEG Challenge (NEMAR nm000290 v1.0.0, CC-BY-4.0), the decoder receives one 4-second EEG window at a time (B = 1, 41 electrodes sampled at 120 hertz, Hz, samples per second, giving 41 x 480 values) and classifies three mental commands: motor imagery (MI, mentally rehearsing a hand movement without moving muscles), mental subtraction (SUB, silently subtracting numbers in working memory), and word generation (WG, silently thinking of words starting with a prompted letter).

Windows arrive in a strict causal online stream inside each recording session (one participant visit of roughly 190 to 218 windows across Graz bar feedback and BrainHero game feedback interfaces): predictions at window t may adapt from windows 0..t of the active session, and reset_state() erases all session state before the next session begins. Accuracy is scored by cell-mean balanced accuracy, the mean per-class recall inside each (session, interface) cell averaged equally across cells.

Plain-Language Neuroscience, Signal-Processing & Machine-Learning Glossary

Every technical term and abbreviation used across the NeurIPS BCI codebase and reports.

Electroencephalography (EEG): Non-invasive recording of microvolt-scale electrical brain activity from scalp electrodes (41 channels at 120 Hz here).
Sensorimotor Rhythm (SMR) & ERD: 8–30 Hz mu/beta oscillations over motor cortex that drop in amplitude (event-related desynchronization) on the hemisphere opposite an imagined limb movement.
Spatial Covariance & Euclidean Alignment (EA): The 41×41 electrode co-variation matrix C_t; multiplying each window by R_t^(-1/2) whitens session-specific skull and impedance scaling.
Pseudo-Chronological Kernel (PseudoChrono): Causal Gaussian similarity weights K_{t,s} (s < t) in a 6-D non-motor drift subspace so adaptation works even when session windows are randomly shuffled (OW5).
Riemannian Tangent Space: Projecting curved symmetric positive-definite covariance matrices onto a flat tangent plane so regularized linear and MLP classifiers can score covariance geometry.
Common Spatial Patterns (CSP) & LDA: Supervised spatial filters maximizing class variance contrast paired with linear discriminant analysis, refitted online at windows 66 and 126.
Batch Normalization (BN) & Tent: Rescaling CNN activations with causal session moments (BN) and nudging BN scale/shift weights to minimize prediction entropy (Tent) up to window 130.
Foundation Model, LoRA & SVD: Pretrained external EEG encoders (EEGPT, CodeBrain, CBraMod, MIRepNet) adapted with rank-8 low-rank matrix factors (LoRA) or compressed via singular value decomposition (SVD).
Sinkhorn-Knopp Cell Balancing: Iterative causal row/column scaling (4 steps) keeping per-window probabilities normalized while equalizing cumulative class totals inside each session cell.
